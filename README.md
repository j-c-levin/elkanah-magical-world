# Elkanah's Magical World

A single-page, offline-friendly playground for a five-year-old who loves mermaids, princesses, unicorns and sparkles. No build step, no dependencies: open `index.html` or visit the GitHub Pages site.

## Places

- **Mermaid Lagoon**: tap fish to make them spin, open shells to find pearls, drag to make bubbles, tap the mermaid to hear her sing.
- **Princess Castle**: dress up Princess Elkanah with dress colours, crowns, hair and skin tones. Twirl for a fanfare, ask the magic mirror for a compliment.
- **Unicorn Meadow**: tap the unicorn to jump, tap apples to feed it, paint a rainbow, switch to night to see the horn glow, and gallop across the field.
- **Sparkle Studio**: finger painting with rainbow, glitter and stamp brushes. Save the picture as a PNG.

Stars collected anywhere land in the sparkle jar, which is saved in the browser. Every ten stars is a celebration.

## Add it to a phone or tablet

It is a progressive web app, so it installs like an app and works offline after the first visit.

- **iPhone or iPad**: open the site in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android**: open the site in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

The icon is the gold star in `icons/`. To change it, edit `icons/icon.svg` and regenerate the PNGs.

## Notes for grown-ups

- Every button speaks its name using the browser's built-in voice, so no reading is needed. The speaker button in the corner mutes both sound effects and speech.
- The talking-head button cycles through the English voices on the device and remembers your pick. The site prefers the most natural female voice it can find. For nicer voices: on iPhone or iPad go to Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, English and download an Enhanced or Premium voice such as Kate, Serena or Samantha. On Windows, Microsoft Edge has the most natural voices built in. Chrome has Google UK English Female when online.
- Works in Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox on desktop, tablet and phone. Landscape is nicest on tablets.
- To run locally, double-click `index.html`.
